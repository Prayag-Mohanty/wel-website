EE 230: Analog Circuits Lab

Operational amplifiers, BJTs and FETs, and feedback systems, measured on the standard WEL bench.

The Analog Circuits Laboratory (EE 230) is a core lab course designed for undergraduate students in Electrical Engineering. It focuses on hands-on experiments with analog circuit components, including operational amplifiers (op-amps), transistors (BJTs and FETs), and feedback systems.
